# Relevance criteria

Relevance criteria

## Score 3 (read now)
A concrete government, parliamentary, or judicial step on something my proposals track:

* Regulation of the National Critical Infrastructure Security Policy (Política Nacional de Segurança de Infraestruturas Críticas) and its national strategy and plan: new decrees, revisions, criteria for identifying which assets count as critical infrastructure, timelines, or delays.
* Bills, provisional measures (medidas provisórias), or decrees updating the National Civil Protection and Defence Policy (Lei 12.608/2012), or creating or publishing the National Civil Protection and Defence Plan (Plano Nacional de Proteção e Defesa Civil).
* Bills or decrees on national cybersecurity, including the National Cybersecurity Policy (PNCiber), the National Cybersecurity Committee, and proposals for a cybersecurity legal framework or a national cybersecurity agency.
* Federal budget decisions on civil defence and disaster risk reduction, including the National Fund for Public Calamities (FUNCAP), extraordinary credits (créditos extraordinários) for disasters, and the treatment of disaster spending under the fiscal framework (arcabouço fiscal) and its spending limits.
* Decisions of the National Governors' Forum, the National Council for Civil Protection and Defence (CONPDEC), or national forums of state civil defence officials on civil defence funding or law.
* Publication of national risk mapping or risk assessments, the national climate adaptation plan, early-warning system plans (including the Defesa Civil Alerta cell broadcast system), or contingency plans for critical infrastructure.
* Federal Court of Accounts (TCU) audit reports and government answers to parliamentary requests for information (requerimentos de informação) on any of the above.
* Supreme Federal Court (STF) rulings or Public Prosecutor's Office (Ministério Público) actions requiring the federal or state governments to act on disaster preparedness or critical infrastructure protection.

Also score 3:

* Substantive analysis or commentary on Brazilian civil defence or critical infrastructure policy.
* Positions from state governments, municipalities, municipal associations (such as CNM or FNP), humanitarian organisations, industry bodies (such as CNI or ABDIB), or expert groups.
* Congressional debate and opposition criticism in the Câmara dos Deputados or Senado Federal.
* Sabotage, cyberattacks, or hybrid attacks on Brazilian infrastructure, including attacks on government systems and power grid, telecommunications, water, port, or dam incidents caused deliberately.

## Score 2 (worth a look)

* Local news: siren tests, emergency drills (simulados), local civil defence units, volunteer programmes, municipal-level announcements.
* General explainers and passing mentions.
* Law-firm and consultancy compliance briefings.

## Score 1 (on-topic but ignorable)

* Items about Portugal, Angola, Mozambique, or other countries with no Brazilian federal, state, or municipal angle.
* Job adverts, public-sector job exams (concursos), product marketing, event listings.
* Items where the search term appears in an unrelated sense.

## Ignore entirely (hidden unless you tick "Ignored")
Nothing. Do not give any item a score of 0.
