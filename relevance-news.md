
# News alert relevance criteria

All High and Medium items must have a clear link to Brazil (Brazilian federal, state, or municipal government, Brazilian institutions, Brazil's role in international forums such as Mercosur, BRICS, the G20, or UN climate negotiations, or Brazil as the primary subject of analysis). Items about other countries with no Brazilian angle are Low. Many alerts are in Portuguese, so check carefully that items are about Brazil and not Portugal, Angola, Mozambique, or other Portuguese-speaking countries.

## High
Policy or government relevance required. Any one of the following qualifies:

- Concrete steps by the Brazilian federal government, the National Congress (Câmara dos Deputados or Senado Federal), state governments, or state legislatures on civil defence (defesa civil) or critical infrastructure: legislation, bills (projetos de lei), decrees, provisional measures (medidas provisórias), ministerial ordinances (portarias), requests for information (requerimentos de informação), and budget decisions.
- Statements or positions from federal ministries and agencies, including the Ministry of Integration and Regional Development (MIDR) and its National Secretariat of Civil Defence (SEDEC), the Institutional Security Office (GSI), the Ministry of Mines and Energy, the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, regulators such as ANEEL, ANATEL, and ANA, and state civil defence agencies, governors, or the National Governors' Forum.
- Publication of official Brazilian documents: national or state risk assessments, civil defence plans and contingency plans, the National Critical Infrastructure Security Policy or Strategy, climate adaptation plans, audit reports from the Federal Court of Accounts (TCU) or state courts of accounts, and official evaluations.
- Sabotage, cyberattacks, hybrid attacks, or major incidents affecting Brazilian critical infrastructure, such as power grid blackouts, attacks on government systems, or failures of dams, ports, telecommunications, or water supply.
- Substantive expert, academic, or think-tank analysis of Brazilian civil defence or critical infrastructure policy.
- Positions from Brazilian industry bodies (such as CNI or ABDIB), humanitarian organisations (such as the Brazilian Red Cross), or municipal associations (such as CNM or FNP) on civil defence law or funding.

## Medium
The search term must be the main subject of the item, and the item must have a clear Brazilian connection. Policy relevance is not required. Any one of the following qualifies:

- Reporting on Brazilian civil defence exercises, emergency drills (simulados), disaster responses, or infrastructure incidents where the event itself is the story.
- Local or regional Brazilian news where the search term is the clear focus.
- Analysis or reporting on how Brazil is affected by or responding to the alert topic (e.g. a risk analysis of Brazil's exposure to floods, droughts, or landslides, a study of Brazil's pandemic preparedness, a report on biosecurity risks in Brazil).
- General explainers or background features about Brazilian civil defence or critical infrastructure where the search term is the central topic.

## Low

- Items where the search term appears in passing or as context for a different story.
- Items about Portugal, other Latin American countries, or any other country with no Brazilian angle.
- Law-firm and consultancy compliance briefings.
- Job adverts, public-sector job exams (concursos), product marketing, event listings, and conference announcements.
- Items where the search term is used in an unrelated sense.
